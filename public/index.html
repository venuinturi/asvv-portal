GOOD TO KNOW

Questions are
a good start.

Here are a few things you might be wondering before we talk.

Do I need a detailed brief to get started?+

Not at all. A description of the problem and the outcome you want is a useful starting point. If you have a brief or existing software, you can share that too.

Can you work on existing software?+

Yes. We can discuss changes, testing or support after understanding the software, your requirements and any access or technical constraints.

How much will my project cost?+

That depends on the scope and complexity. Once we understand your requirements, we can discuss a suitable proposal and commercial terms.

Can you help after launch?+

Yes. We can discuss fixes, updates and further improvements under a separately agreed support arrangement.
